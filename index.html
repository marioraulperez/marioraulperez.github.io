About

I spent years shipping software that had to be correct: tax invoicing, health records, cross-border systems, identity. Over the last two years I’ve been building with AI as the default way of working, not as a feature tacked on at the end.

Today I’m a founding engineer at Cora. I design the agent platform and the agents that run on it. The job is to turn messy post-sales work into systems that actually execute: tools, evals, human-in-the-loop, and the data layer underneath.

The bottleneck is no longer writing code. It’s deciding what should exist, then making it reliable.

Cora

Founding Engineer · 2025-present

Cora is a platform of proactive AI agents for customer success. Teams connect CRM, email, calendar, calls, and Slack. Cora watches accounts, then runs agents on a schedule or when something happens, to do the work a CSM would otherwise do by hand.

I work both sides of that system: the platform (runtime, skills, signals, evals, permissions, data) and the deployments (taking a new account from zero to steady state). Platform without customers is a demo. Agents without a platform don’t survive production.

Approach

Decide what should exist

I start from the operation, not the model. If the process is unclear, an agent will only fail faster.

Make it reliable

Tools, evals, permissions, and a human brake. Production agents need the same seriousness as tax and health software.

Close the loop

Deploy with the customer, watch what breaks, feed it back into the platform. That’s the job.
